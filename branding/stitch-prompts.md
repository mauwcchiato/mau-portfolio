# Mauwcchiato: branding prompts (for Google Stitch)

**Mauwcchiato** = Mau (my nickname) + mauw (a cat's meow) + macchiato (coffee).
The mascot is a cute soft-3D cartoon version of me, peeking over a laptop with `</>` on it,
always with a macchiato mug nearby and sometimes a small purple cat.
The pink **w** in the wordmark is the cat's smile (·ω·): a rounded, two-bump w with short whiskers
on each side. It's the "mauw" in the name, and it's where the cat sits inside the word.
My real name, Jhan Maurice De Roxas, stays the main title on the site; Mauwcchiato is the brand and handle.

Copy one prompt at a time. Each one repeats the style rules, so it works on its own.
If a result is close, reply with a short fix ("keep everything, make the glasses rounder")
instead of starting over. Use the SAME mascot description every time so the character stays consistent.

## Style rules (repeated inside each prompt)

- Style: soft 3D cartoon illustration, like a cosy modern app mascot. Chibi proportions (big head,
  small body), smooth clay-like shading, soft rounded shapes, gentle warm lighting, no harsh outlines.
- The mascot (always the same): a young person with shoulder-length straight hair in caramel/honey
  brown with slightly darker roots and a centre part, round thin-rimmed clear glasses, warm light-tan
  skin, big friendly dark eyes behind the glasses, soft rosy cheeks, a small calm smile, wearing a
  cosy oversized purple #7C3AED hoodie.
- Colours: purple #7C3AED, light purple #A78BFA, pink #E879F9, cream #FFF4E6, coffee brown #8B5E3C,
  near-black #14101F, soft lavender background #F5F2FB.
- Recurring props: a laptop with a glowing pink "</>" logo on the lid, a cream mug of macchiato with
  a "</>" latte art or print, a small round purple cat.
- Fonts (for text in layouts): Space Grotesk Bold for "Mauwcchiato" (title case, not all caps; the same font as the website headings),
  Inter for body text, JetBrains Mono for small labels.

## Not for Stitch: your professional photo

AI shouldn't make this one; it needs to be really you. Tips for a good one on a phone:

- Face a window for soft daylight. Avoid overhead lights and flash.
- Plain wall or simple background; stand an arm's length away from it.
- Phone at eye level (not from above), back camera, portrait mode, 2× zoom.
- Head and shoulders, slight smile, look at the camera.
- Take a lot and pick the sharpest. Keep the original full size (at least 1200×1500).
