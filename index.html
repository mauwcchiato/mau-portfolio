About me

Two jobs on the same project

01 / 11

On paper I keep ending up as the project manager. In practice I never step back from the code. On D.I.L.I.G. I led the work and wrote the Kotlin app, the PHP API, and the Arduino firmware. On Kusina Kode I run the schedule and own the game engine.

I like the seams: the ESP8266 talking to PHP, a Compose ViewModel talking to MySQL. That is where projects actually fail, and it is the part I want to get right. I'm looking for an OJT placement where I can build the thing and keep the build legible to everyone on it.
